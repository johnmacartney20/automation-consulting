# Automation ROI Calculator Guide

## Purpose
This guide helps you calculate the Return on Investment (ROI) for automation projects, enabling data-driven decisions about efficiency improvements.

---

## Quick ROI Formula

```
ROI = (Annual Benefits - Annual Costs) / Total Implementation Cost × 100%

Payback Period = Total Implementation Cost / Annual Net Benefit
```

## Example ROI Calculation

### Example: Email Automation for Customer Service Team

**Current State:**
- 5 agents spend 10 hours/week on repetitive emails
- Labor cost: 50 hours × $25/hr × 52 weeks = $65,000/year
- Error rate: 5%, costing $5,000/year
- **Total Current Cost: $70,000/year**

**Implementation Cost:**
- Email automation platform: $5,000
- Setup and configuration: $3,000
- Training: $2,000
- **Total Implementation: $10,000**

**Annual Recurring Cost:**
- Software subscription: $3,600/year

**Expected Benefits:**
- 80% time savings: 40 hours × $25 × 52 = $52,000/year
- 95% error reduction: $4,750/year
- Faster response times → 10% customer retention improvement: $15,000/year
- **Total Annual Benefits: $71,750/year**

**ROI Calculation:**
- Net Annual Benefit: $71,750 - $3,600 = $68,150
- ROI: ($68,150 / $10,000) × 100% = **681%**
- Payback Period: $10,000 / $68,150 = **1.8 months**
- 3-Year NPV: $159,450

**Decision: Strong Go** ✓

## Best Practices

### 1. Be Conservative with Estimates
- Underestimate benefits by 20-30%
- Overestimate costs by 10-20%
- Include contingency buffers

### 2. Include All Costs
- Don't forget training, change management, ongoing support
- Consider opportunity cost of team time

### 3. Measure Actual Results
- Track KPIs before and after implementation
- Adjust projections based on real data
- Report on ROI achievement

### 4. Consider Timeframes
- Quick wins (< 3 month payback) should be prioritized
- Strategic initiatives may have longer payback but higher NPV

### 5. Account for Risks
- Run multiple scenarios
- Identify and mitigate key risks
- Have contingency plans

### 6. Document Assumptions
- Record all assumptions made in calculations
- Review and update as new information emerges
- Share with stakeholders for validation
